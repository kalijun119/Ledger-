Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7/19/26

This is the Privacy Policy page covering topics for an intended-to-be-small site like this one — it is not legal advice.

1. Information we collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and password (which is scrambled with bcrypt before being stored — we never store or can see your actual password). We also store the date you joined and, temporarily, your login session.

2. How we use it

Your information is used only to: let you log in and stay logged in, identify your account for support requests, and enforce security measures like login-attempt limits. We do not sell your information, and we don't share it with advertisers.

3. Cookies and sessions

We use a single session cookie to keep you logged in. It doesn't track you across other websites and isn't used for advertising.

4. Third-party services

We use Finnhub and Twelve Data to fetch market data. These requests are made from our server, not your browser, and don't include your personal information — they're simply "what's the price of AAPL" style requests, unconnected to your identity.

5. How we protect your data

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt before storage. Login attempts are rate-limited, and an account locks temporarily after repeated failed attempts. Security headers (via Helmet) are enabled on our server. No security measure is perfect, but these are standard, meaningful protections.

6. How long we keep your data

We keep your account information until you delete it. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from the Account page — this takes effect immediately.

7. Children's privacy

Ledger is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

8. Your rights

You can view and update your account details, change your password, and delete your account at any time from the Account page.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes meaningfully, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
