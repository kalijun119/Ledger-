Move signals

What the trend indicators say right now

This page is not financial advice, and no one — including Ledger — can reliably predict short-term market moves. "Add," "Trim," and "Hold" below are plain-language labels for standard trend indicators (moving-average position, momentum, and volatility) computed from the price history shown elsewhere on this site. They describe where a stock has recently been, not where it will go. Markets are affected by news, earnings, and events no indicator can see coming. Always do your own research and consider talking to a licensed financial advisor before moving money.

How a signal is built

1. Trend

Short vs. long average

Compares a 5-point average to a 20-point average. Short-term above long-term is read as firming; the reverse is read as softening.

2. Momentum

Recent direction

Looks at how consistently the last several moves pointed the same way, as a rough stand-in for momentum.

3. Volatility

How much it swings

Wider recent swings lower the confidence score, since a noisy trend is a less reliable one.
